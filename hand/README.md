# Handwritten verification

The course requires at least one photograph of a derivation completed by the
student. This repository does not fabricate that evidence.

Recommended check: derive

$$
\frac{d\operatorname{Var}[V(\theta)]}{d\theta}
=a_0+2\theta\operatorname{Var}(\Gamma/s)
$$

and verify that condition (30) implies a negative slope at zero but not a
positive slope at one. Add the resulting phone photograph here as
`variance-audit.jpg`, then reference it in the two oral-exam slides the student
will complete later.
