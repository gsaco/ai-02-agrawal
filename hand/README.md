# Handwritten verification

The course requires at least one photograph of a derivation completed by the
student. This repository does not fabricate that evidence.

Recommended check: derive the slope of the cross-sectional variance of
$V(\theta)$. Show that it equals $a_{0}+2\theta$ times the variance of
$\Gamma/s$, and verify that condition (30) implies a negative slope at zero but
not a positive slope at one. Add the resulting phone photograph here as
`variance-audit.jpg`, then reference it in the two oral-exam slides the student
will complete later.
